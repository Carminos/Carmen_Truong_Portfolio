01 — Profile

A technical grounding, applied to real decisions

I work at the intersection of engineering and economics — assessing where power limitations sit, what a sustainable transition actually costs, and how to explain that clearly to the people who have to decide. From my experience at Hoare Lea, that's meant technoeconomic assessments across 10+ projects in commercial, industrial, logistics, renewable generation and data centre settings, coordinating delivery on four unique projects alongside engineers and stakeholders of varied disciplines.

Before that, I spent time at Skanska on the delivery side of building services, FM and a phased data centre delivery.

My education is in Architectural Engineering which has taught me the principles of building engineering (thermodynamics, civils, structural, electrical) from the perspective of people - making careful considerations for the impacts of engineering decisions to our interaction with the built environment.
